The publication

An open console for technical knowledge.

Purpose

Document technology from the perspective of the people who design, operate, maintain and recover it. The publication is written for mid-senior and senior professionals, specialists, architects and technical leaders.

It does not aim to cover every announcement or turn every vendor release into a news story. It prioritises changes with operational consequences, procedures worth understanding and investigations that improve technical decisions.

Technical approach

Coverage includes systems and infrastructure, IT operations, observability, continuity and recovery, automation, platform engineering, software and the application of AI to IT.

The value of a technical piece lies in its context, limitations, risks and the evidence that allows readers to assess its conclusions.

Editorial principles

  • Technical claims are checked against primary documentation wherever possible.
  • Reproduced tests are labelled as lab work and identify the test environment.
  • Postmortems are attributed to their authors; editorial analysis is kept distinct from published facts.
  • Commands include context, privileges, risks and validation.
  • Confidential customer, employer or private-environment data is never published.

Founder and editor

Consola Abierta is an independent initiative founded and edited by Ray Vega. It is built as a personal technical knowledge project: research, reproduce where possible, document carefully and share what has been learned.

For public corrections and editorial suggestions, use the editorial contact page. Do not include personal or confidential information in public issues. For private matters and data-protection requests, email contacto@consolaabierta.com.

The editorial policy evolves with the publication. Material corrections should be identified on the affected content.
