Editorial contact

Public channel for editorial corrections and suggestions to Consola Abierta.

Public corrections and suggestions

For corrections, errata and editorial suggestions, use the public issue tracker of the project repository. A GitHub account is required.

Open a public issue on GitHub ↗

Issues are public and may be indexed. Do not include personal information, confidential material, customer or employer data, credentials or security-sensitive details.

Private matters and data protection requests

Do not use public issues for private correspondence, legal notices or data-subject rights requests. A private editorial email address has not yet been configured, so these requests cannot currently be handled through this public channel.

This page describes the public channel that is available today. It does not offer private support or promise a response time.
