Privacy and Cookie Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026. This policy describes the website and the processing associated with hosting and advertising services that may be used.

1. Website operator and contact

Consola Abierta is an independent technical publication. For questions about privacy, cookies or data-subject rights, contact contacto@consolaabierta.com. The publisher's full legal identity and address must be stated in the site's legal notice; those details are still awaiting confirmation by the publisher.

2. Data that may be processed

The website provides static editorial pages. Catalogue search runs in the visitor's browser against a static JSON file; search terms are not sent to the publisher or stored in a first-party system.

When the website is accessed, the hosting provider may process technical data needed to deliver and protect the pages, such as IP address, request date and time, requested URL, browser information and security logs. The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Advertising and Google AdSense

Consola Abierta has integrated the Google AdSense code and submitted the site for review. The display of ads is subject to Google's approval and to the activation of ad formats in the account.

When ads are displayed, Google and the relevant advertising technology providers may use cookies, similar identifiers, IP addresses, browser and device information, and signals about interaction with ads. These technologies may be used to display ads, limit their frequency, measure performance, prevent fraud and, where valid consent or another permitted legal basis applies, personalise advertising.

Third parties serving ads on this website may set and read cookies in the user's browser, use web beacons, or process IP addresses and other identifiers as a result of ad serving. The current list of advertising technology providers and the purposes for which consent is requested will be shown in Google's consent message and its options panel.

Further information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google advertising.

4. Cookies and privacy choices

Cookies and similar technologies may store or access information on a device. Strictly necessary cookies used for functionality and security are distinct from advertising or measurement technologies that require information and, where applicable, prior consent.

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Consola Abierta intends to use the Google AdSense Privacy & Messaging consent message, integrated with the IAB TCF framework, to identify providers and let users consent, refuse or manage their choices. Refusal should not be hidden or require more steps than acceptance.

Once the CMP is published, users will be able to reopen the panel through the “Cookie & privacy settings” link in the footer of compatible pages. Consent can be withdrawn or changed through that panel. The panel depends on the European message being published and correctly loaded from AdSense.

5. Recipients and international transfers

Technical access data may be processed by GitHub as hosting provider. Data associated with advertising may be processed by Google Ireland Limited, Google LLC and the advertising technology providers identified in the consent message, depending on the settings and the user's choices. These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area under the mechanisms and safeguards described in their respective policies.

6. Legal bases and retention

The legal basis for non-essential cookies and personalised advertising will be consent where required by law. Processing strictly necessary to deliver and secure the website is based on the hosting provider's and publisher's legitimate interest in maintaining availability and security. Emails received will be processed to respond to the enquiry and retained for as long as necessary to handle it and address potential legal responsibilities.

Specific retention periods for data processed by Google, GitHub and advertising providers are governed by their respective policies and settings. Consola Abierta does not control those providers' internal retention periods.

7. Data-subject rights

Where data protection law applies, users may request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and may withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. To exercise these rights or ask a question, contact contacto@consolaabierta.com. Users may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

8. External links and changes

Pages may contain links to third-party services. Their operators are responsible for their own websites and policies. This policy may be updated when services, providers, technologies or legal requirements change. The update date will appear at the beginning of the document.

9. Important information still to be completed

Before ads are actively served, the publisher must complete and publish the legal identity and address required in the legal notice, confirm the data controller details and test the European consent message in Spanish and English. This page describes intended advertising technologies; it is not a legal compliance certification.
